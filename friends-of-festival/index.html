Friends of Festival

Support the Book Brahma Literature Festival – Soul of South

Join us in celebrating the rich literary and cultural heritage of South India by becoming a Friend of the Festival. Your support is vital in helping us bring together authors, readers, and cultural enthusiasts from across the region.

Why Become a Friend of the Festival?

The Book Brahma Literature Festival – Soul of South is more than just an event; it’s a movement to preserve and promote the vibrant languages and cultures of Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, and Malayalam. By becoming a Friend of the Festival, you are contributing to a cause that fosters a love for literature, culture, and community.

Your Impact

  • Cultural Enrichment: Your contribution will help us host renowned authors, organize cultural performances, and offer interactive workshops.
  • Community Engagement: Support the largest literary festival dedicated to South Indian languages, expected to attract over 90,000 attendees from all five South Indian states.
  • Preservation of Heritage: Play a crucial role in preserving and promoting the literary traditions of South India for future generations.

Benefits of Being a Friend of the Festival

  • Exclusive access and updates: Enjoy exclusive all-entry VIP passes for you and your family, granting access to every event. Stay updated with the latest festival news and announcements.
  • Acknowledgment: Your support will be recognized at the festival.
  • 80G Receipt: Contributions are tax-exempt under Section 80G, ensuring you can support a good cause while enjoying tax benefits.
