About Book Brahma Foundation

Book Brahma Foundation, a registered charitable trust, promotes literature, art, and culture across South Indian languages. Its “Book Brahma Literature Festival- 2024” united South Indian languages, fostering dialogue and exchange. Committed to primary education and language preservation, the foundation aims to instill literary passion in future generations while digitally safeguarding India’s rich literary heritage.

The Festival Family Speaks.....

D. V. Guruprasad, Indian Author and former DG of Police, Government of Karnataka, Bengaluru. He majorly writes in Kannada and English.

The Best-Organized Literature Festival

Having attended many literary festivals in India and abroad, I can unhesitatingly say that the Book Brahma Lit fest is one of the best-organized festivals. Being the first festival dedicated to four South Indian languages presents both a challenge and an opportunity. The first such festival faced various challenges and succeeded in bringing together authors and publishers from many languages. The themes for the fest were chosen carefully, and they could attract large crowds. I wish the second edition all the success.

– K. Satchidanandan, renowned Indian Poet and Author; Chairman of Kerala Sahitya Academy, Thrissur, Kerala. He writes in Malayalam.

Great Pleasure to be Part of #BBLF2024

I am thrilled that #BBLF is holding its second edition on the 8th, 9th, and 10th of August 2025 at St. John’s Auditorium in Bengaluru. As a participant in the first edition, it brings together representatives of all the major languages of South India. When I say significant, I don’t mean only the four languages but also the languages spoken by collectives, tribes, and small communities, which contribute to growth and quality from year to year. It is a great pleasure to be in Bengaluru during those days. I am sure there will be many more writers this time, including those not represented in the last edition. I wish BBLF-2 all the best.

– K. Satchidanandan, renowned Indian Poet and Author; Chairman of Kerala Sahitya Academy, Thrissur, Kerala. He writes in Malayalam.
